Density-fitting setup cost: scheduler and timer records for two Phoenix jobs
============================================================================

Behind the "What the J/K timer does not cover" section of
doc/sphinxman/source/gtfock.rst. Two jobs, both one process on one exclusive
core24 node, both re-running only the ``df`` arm so that the cost
``JK::initialize()`` pays outside ``timer_on("JK: JK")`` could be read off
directly:

  12638902  gtfdf-setup   four points at 160 GB: 260, 574, 1555 and 1863 bf
  12639820  gtfdf-setup2  a discarded warm-up, then 260 and 574 bf at 120 GB
                          and 1863 bf at 185 GB

The second job exists because of two things the first one showed. Its 260-bf
point took 27.5 s against 3.3 s in every earlier sweep, and the timer tree below
puts 21.172 s of that in ``HF: DIIS`` -- PSIO scratch I/O on cold Lustre, paid
once by whichever process runs first, and absorbed in the second job by a
throwaway warm-up point. And its 1863-bf point silently changed algorithm: the
MemDFJK memory estimate did not fit the budget, so Psi4 fell back to DiskDFJK
(the two lines quoted below), which does not use DFHelper and so reported a
setup cost of zero through instrumentation that only watched ``DFH:
initialize()``. Raising the budget to 185 GB in the second job kept the same
point in core, and the two builders are reported side by side rather than one
being chosen.

The citable per-point split is the timer tree, not the JSON record: the driver
at the time these jobs ran captured only ``DFH: initialize()``, so its
``df_setup_total_seconds`` field omits ``DFH: sparsity prep`` everywhere and all
of DiskDFJK's setup at 1863 bf. tests/pytests/gtfock_hpc_benchmark.py has since
been widened to capture both builders' names; the numbers in gtfock.rst are the
widened totals, summed from the trees below.

results/dfsetup_12638902/protein157_1863/protein157_1863/psi4_rank0.out:255-256

  MemDFJK Memory: AOs need 127.825 GiB; user supplied 111.759 GiB.
  MemDFJK requires too much memory. DiskDFJK algorithm will be used.

No such pair appears in any other point of either job, so every other row below
is MemDFJK.
