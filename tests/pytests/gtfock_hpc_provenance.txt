GTFock rank-scaling sweep: scheduler records for the two Phoenix jobs
====================================================================

Copied verbatim from the run artifacts of the two jobs behind the measured
tables in doc/sphinxman/source/gtfock.rst: job 12400108 (peptide) and job
12400109 (nanotube). For each job, in order: the run's own provenance block,
the sacct query for the job (whose MaxRSS and MaxVMSize columns come back empty
on this cluster), and the SLURM epilogue block PACE appends to the job log after
the batch script has already exited.

The epilogue's "Rsrc Used: ... mem=" field is the citable source for the two
job-level memory figures quoted in gtfock.rst. Every per-point number in that
document comes from gtfock_hpc_results.csv instead.
